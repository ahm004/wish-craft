We collect only what's needed to make and show your card. We don't sell your data, show ads, or use tracking cookies.

This policy explains how {{legalName}} ("we"), operator of {{brand}} at {{website}}, handles personal data, in line with the Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023 and the Information Technology Act, 2000.

1. What we collect

  • Card content you enter: the recipient's name, your name, your message, the question (for proposals and similar cards), photo captions and your photos. Music is built in: we never ask for, upload or store songs.
  • Payment details: payments are handled by Razorpay. We receive only the order ID, payment ID, amount and payment status. We never receive your card number, CVV, UPI PIN or bank login.
  • Usage: how many times a card has been opened, and when it was first opened.
  • Technical data: our hosting providers automatically log basic technical data such as IP address, browser type and time of request, for security and to keep the service running.
  • Stored in your own browser: to show the "Your cards" list, your browser saves your card IDs and a private access code. This stays on your device and is not used for tracking.

2. How we use it

  • to create, store and display your card to people who have its link;
  • to process and confirm your payment;
  • to show you whether your card is paid and how often it was opened;
  • to answer your support and refund requests;
  • to prevent fraud and misuse, and to meet legal obligations.

We process this data because you ask us to provide the service by creating and paying for a card.

3. Who can see your card

Anyone with your card's link can see its content, including the names, message and photos. Share the link only with people you trust.

4. Service providers we use

We share data only with providers who help us run {{brand}}:

  • Razorpay processes payments (see razorpay.com/privacy).
  • Supabase stores card details and photos until they are deleted (see section 5).
  • Vercel hosts the website and server code.
  • Google Fonts provides the fonts. Your browser loads them from Google's servers, which can see your IP address.

These providers may store or process data on servers outside India. We don't sell or rent your personal data to anyone.

5. How long we keep it

  • Paid cards are kept for {{cardLifetime}} after payment so your link keeps working. After that, the card, its text and its photos are deleted automatically and the link stops working.
  • Unpaid cards and their photos are deleted automatically after 24 hours.
  • Payment records are kept as long as required by tax and accounting laws.

6. Your rights

You can ask us to:

  • tell you what personal data we hold about your card;
  • correct it;
  • delete your card and its photos sooner (they are deleted automatically after {{cardLifetime}} anyway);
  • handle a complaint about how we process your data.

Email {{email}} with your card link and we'll respond within {{responseDays}} working days. To protect your card, we may ask you to confirm you are its creator, for example by sharing the Razorpay payment ID.

If someone has put your photo or details in a card without your consent, write to us and we'll review it promptly.

7. Security

Card data is sent over encrypted HTTPS connections. Photos are stored privately and shown only through temporary links for paid cards, and our database is not accessible from the browser. No online service is 100% secure, but we take reasonable steps to protect your data.
