In short: preview is free, so please check your card before paying. Because your link is delivered instantly, payments are non-refundable, except when something goes wrong on our side. In that case you get a full refund.

1. When you get a full refund

We refund the full ₹{{price}} if:

  • You were charged but didn't get your card link, and we can't deliver it within 24 hours of you contacting us.
  • You were charged more than once for the same card.
  • Your card doesn't open or work properly because of a problem on our side, and we can't fix it within 3 working days of you reporting it.

2. When refunds are not given

  • You changed your mind after the link was delivered.
  • There is a spelling mistake or wrong detail that you entered (please check the free preview first).
  • The recipient didn't open the card, or didn't like it.
  • The card has expired. Cards are deleted automatically {{cardLifetime}} after payment, so please report any problem within that time.
  • The card was removed for breaking our Terms & Conditions.
  • The problem is caused by the recipient's device, browser or internet connection, and the card works normally on other devices.

3. How to request a refund

Email {{email}} within {{refundDays}} days of payment with:

  • your card link, if you have it;
  • the Razorpay payment ID (starts with pay_, found in your payment receipt, email or SMS);
  • the date of payment and a short description of the problem (screenshots help).

We'll reply within {{responseDays}} working days.

4. How refunds are paid

Approved refunds are started within 5–7 working days and go back to the original payment method (the same UPI account, card, bank account or wallet). Depending on your bank, it can take a few extra days to appear in your account.

5. Cancellation

  • Before payment: you can stop at any time just by closing the page or the payment window. Nothing is charged. Unpaid cards are deleted automatically.
  • After payment: the order can't be cancelled, because the card link is delivered immediately. You can still ask us to delete your card at any time. Deleting a card doesn't come with a refund unless one of the cases in section 1 applies.

6. Failed payments

If money was deducted but the payment failed or wasn't confirmed, it is usually reversed automatically by Razorpay or your bank within 5–7 working days. If it isn't, contact us with the payment ID and we'll help.
